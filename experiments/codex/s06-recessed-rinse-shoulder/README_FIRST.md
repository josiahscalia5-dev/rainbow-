## Play or edit

- **Android** — `playable/Rainbow_Screen06_Recessed_Shoulder_Candidate.apk`. Choose the stage entrance for a complete steerable ride.
- **Browser** — `playable/Rainbow_Screen06_Recessed_Shoulder_Playable_Web.zip`. Serving instructions are inside.
- **Editable game** — `playable/Rainbow_Screen06_Editable_Game.zip` contains TypeScript, runtime assets, original references, Android sources, scripts, Blender utilities and the dependency lockfile.
- **Editable Blender scene** — `editable/Screen06_Recessed_Shoulder.blend`. Its opening, +3 and +6 cameras came from the running game. Its meshes and UVs were verified against the runtime export.
- **Reproduction and findings** — `RESULT.md`, `RESTORE_AND_CAPTURE.md`, `checks/`, `MIRA_FEASIBILITY.md`, `checkpoint/` and the source overlay. The earlier local QA report has its own recovery copy.

The original approved images for all five playable screens decoded at full size and matched the project’s SHA-256 manifest. No other playable screen was edited. The Google Flow masters remain in the separate video workflow. Approval of additional moving viewpoints and promotion of this experiment are decisions for the owner.
