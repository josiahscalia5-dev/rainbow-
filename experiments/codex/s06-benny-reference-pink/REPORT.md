## What changed from Claudia’s Benny

The earlier layered-coat candidate established actual fur relief on the body. Its upper coat was peach and the retained arms were much finer and brighter. The new dye uses three measured luminance ranges from the original and the real game. A second lighting check separated the pale shoulder values from the lower water shadow. Those adjustments use Benny’s own three-dimensional coordinates, so the coat follows his swim and leans naturally. A smaller texture scale on the hands keeps the locks proportional. The pom uses its own purple tuft swatch photographed in the source. The white crown stitches come from a different original region clear of the star.

In a shared patch of visible torso fur, Claudia’s median color is **RGB 255, 50, 188**. The earlier layered-coat candidate measured **245, 105, 172**. This new trial measures **221, 73, 159**, against **229, 71, 166** in the artwork. The native-pixel mean absolute RGB error in that patch falls from **34.13 to 26.31** on a 0–255 scale. Over the union of the old and new visible character silhouettes, the mean error falls from **77.51 to 49.48**. Those are color and registration measurements for the original camera.
