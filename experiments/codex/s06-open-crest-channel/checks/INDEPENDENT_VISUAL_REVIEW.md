# Independent visual review of the playable Screen 06 candidate

I compared the source artwork, Claudia’s retained game, the earlier arched-passage candidate and the new game-rendered frames. Full-size opening renders use 864 × 1,536 pixels. Movement images use the same camera state, course position and clock across versions. The exported movie was decoded through all 63 frames at 12 fps.

| View | Finding |
|---|---|
| Opening | The original cyan-magenta crest, purple patch beneath the coin arc and right-hand passage remain recognizable. The candidate preserves this composition. The retained projection still supplies most of the appearance of the purple opening here; the new recessed purple face itself contributes zero exposed pixels in the 270 × 480 diagnostic. |
| +3 units | The new glossy lip rises to the left behind the gold coin path. Near water and Benny cover most of the opening; it is hard to identify as a distinct gap. The new slotted face contributes 41 exposed diagnostic pixels. This checkpoint falls short. |
| +6 units | A curved, cyan-magenta crest frames a visible wedge of perforated purple metal above the continuous near river. This is an improvement over the earlier solid sheet. The perforations are too large, and the arch partially overlaps the beanie. The new slotted face contributes 1,373 exposed diagnostic pixels. |
| Hard turns | The curl and purple wall stay in the world and Benny is visibly swimming on water. The blunt end and large slots make the wall resemble a projecting bulkhead. |
| Deep bend / exit | The earlier arched-passage bend returns after the new shoulder passes out of view. The ride reaches Screen 07. Near and far materials still differ in detail. |

In the uninterrupted steering ride, the new curl and slotted face are clear around 1.50 seconds. From roughly 2.17 to 3.33 seconds its blunt end and oversized holes stand out. At about 3.67 seconds the shoulder passes left of Benny and the inherited deep bend resumes. Exact image similarity is undefined at these moving views because there is only one owner-approved Screen 06 viewpoint.

The opening metric counts full-frame pixels for which the mean absolute difference between the three encoded RGB channel values is at most 20, including HUD and Benny. Claudia scores 79.0857%, the previous arched-passage candidate 79.0987%, and this candidate 79.1050% on this test. It establishes opening preservation, not exact perceptual similarity.

**Disposition:** partial candidate for owner review. The moving arch improves at +6 and during steering, but +3 and the visible ends of the structure still miss the requested appearance. Keep the retained game protected and defer applying this construction to other screens. For a different follow-up approach, fit the two actual water shoulders and recessed tunnel wall against both moving-camera sightlines, with clearance constraints for the fixed steering lane. Show those proposed moving views to the owner before treating them as reference artwork.
