## Cuberta: facts from cuberta.com and wandy3d.com

**Pages read:** https://cuberta.com/, https://wandy3d.com/cuberta, https://cuberta.com/llms.txt, https://cuberta.com/specs/, https://cuberta.com/faq/, https://cuberta.com/how-it-works/, https://wandy3d.com/privacy (last updated November 18, 2026), https://wandy3d.com/terms (last updated November 12, 2026). Nothing was downloaded or signed up for. The current version is 1.0.2 for Windows and 1.0.1 for macOS (https://cuberta.com/specs/).

### MCP and agents
- Cuberta "exposes a Model Context Protocol server" and "anything that speaks MCP can drive the scene" (https://cuberta.com/llms.txt, https://cuberta.com/faq/). Claude Code is the usual start, and GPT or any MCP client also works (https://wandy3d.com/cuberta).
- Setup: install, open Cuberta, press "Copy connect command" in the top right, and paste it into the terminal where your agent runs (https://cuberta.com/how-it-works/). The actual command text is not stated.
- You bring your own agent and model access (https://cuberta.com/specs/).
- **Not stated:** the MCP tool names, parameters, or any tool-reference docs page. The site's llms.txt and nav list no docs page. Blog posts that may describe the tool surface were not fetched: https://cuberta.com/blog/mcp-servers-for-3d-and-game-dev/ and https://cuberta.com/blog/claude-code-3d-modeling/.

### Platform and headless use
- Windows: 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, installer or portable build. macOS: Apple Silicon or Intel. Any GPU from the last decade (https://cuberta.com/specs/).
- **Linux is not listed as supported.**
- The site describes a desktop app with a viewport and says the agent's work "appears in the viewport as it happens" (https://cuberta.com/). Headless operation is **not stated**.
- Editor and bundled content work offline. The agent needs its own model connection (https://cuberta.com/faq/).
- Builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen or macOS will ask for confirmation on first launch (https://cuberta.com/specs/).

### Geometry
- Stated: road networks with junctions and sidewalks, building blocks with facades, furnished interiors, single rooms or props, terrain, vegetation, street furniture, and lighting and sky (https://cuberta.com/faq/, https://cuberta.com/features/ as linked from https://cuberta.com/).
- Stated: "Hills, vegetation, sky and the time of day" (https://cuberta.com/).
- **Not stated:** curved tubes or tunnels, arbitrary custom meshes built by the agent, and whether roads and buildings are procedurally generated.

### Importing images and meshes
- Wandy3D library and community models can be dragged into the scene (https://wandy3d.com/cuberta).
- Turning a photo into a 3D model inside Cuberta is stated. It runs on Wandy3D and uses credits (https://wandy3d.com/cuberta).
- **Not stated:** importing a reference image or a GLB, OBJ, or FBX file from disk.

### Textures and materials
- Bundled models, materials and skies are included (https://cuberta.com/faq/).
- Exports include textures (https://cuberta.com/specs/).
- **Not stated:** applying your own image texture to geometry.

### Export
- GLB and FBX, with textures included (https://cuberta.com/specs/).
- Unity: FBX imports as-is. GLB needs the glTFast package. Unreal: FBX imports as-is. GLB uses the built-in glTF importer. Godot: GLB is preferred. Blender: File → Import → glTF 2.0 or FBX (https://cuberta.com/how-it-works/).
- **Not stated:** OBJ export.

### Pricing and limits
- Cuberta is free: "no account, no trial, no subscription" (https://cuberta.com/faq/, https://cuberta.com/specs/).
- Sign-in with Wandy3D is optional, needed only to browse your library and community models (https://cuberta.com/specs/).
- Generating models in Cuberta costs Wandy3D credits (https://wandy3d.com/cuberta).
- Downloads are free inside Cuberta, even on the free plan. On wandy3d.com, downloads require PRO (https://wandy3d.com/cuberta).
- **Not stated:** any scene size, polygon, or usage limits in Cuberta. Agent usage is billed to your own model provider.

### Privacy
- **There is no Cuberta-specific privacy policy in what I read.** The Wandy3D policy at https://wandy3d.com/privacy describes its "Services" as the website, "our apps, and our API and integrations". It does not name Cuberta.
- Section 16 covers AI assistants connected to a Wandy3D account. A connected assistant "receives only what its requests return". Models an assistant makes are processed "like an upload on the website".
- Generation uses subprocessors including OpenAI, Anthropic, RunPod, Cloudflare, and Contabo (section 5.1).
- AI-improvement training is **on by default** for uploaded inputs, prompts, and outputs. It applies to free and paid plans. You can turn it off in Profile → Privacy. Requests made with an API key are never used for training.
- Retention for models and inputs: until deleted, and files are erased within 30 days of deletion.
- **Not stated:** whether a scene you build locally in the desktop editor is uploaded or stored. Also not stated: whether any local-only data leaves the machine.

### Terms and ownership
- Terms: https://wandy3d.com/terms.
- Paid Outputs, meaning outputs created on an active paid plan or with a model unlock, "belong to you". They may be used "in games, apps, films, ads, renders, prints and products for sale" (section 4.2).
- Free Outputs "belong to Wandy3D". You get a personal, non-commercial licence (section 4.2).
- Section 3.3 says downloading or exporting model files "requires an active paid plan or a model unlock". This conflicts with the Cuberta claim that downloads are free on the free plan. Section 3.3 also says "maker files" do not need a plan. The terms do not say how this applies to Cuberta.
- **Not stated:** whether a scene the agent builds in Cuberta from primitives or procedural geometry counts as a Wandy3D "Output". This determines whether commercial game use is covered, so it needs a direct answer from the vendor.
- AI outputs "may not be protected by copyright" in every country, and no warranty is given (section 4.2, section 15).

### Other links on the sites
- Blog articles on Unity, Unreal, Godot, and GLB vs FBX are linked from https://cuberta.com/how-it-works/ and https://cuberta.com/llms.txt. I did not fetch them.
- https://wandy3d.com/support, https://wandy3d.com/acceptable-use, and https://wandy3d.com/developers are linked from the Wandy3D pages. I did not fetch them.
