# Mira-Scene feasibility on the Rainbow workspace

Checked on November 19, 2026. The published code and model card were accessible. The official repository was at `ddfe6de1d2ed1d23745b8852d70fcd515693d856`, and the public Mira-Scene weights were at `e0bc99eec6ec6e2a22b81a8af1f323dd07dbdde6`. The repository license and weight card give MIT terms. External models and backends have their own terms and access conditions. [Code](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/Mira-Scene) · [Weights](https://huggingface.co/Yang-Tian/Mira-Scene)

This host exposed no NVIDIA device, CUDA compiler or measurable GPU VRAM. Python 3.12 had no PyTorch, Diffusers, Transformers or nvdiffrast installed. No Conda executable was available. Free disk measured about 42 MiB initially and about 835 MiB after sharing byte-identical disposable build files. The three Mira-CCM inference weights listed by the model host total 6,412,717,052 bytes before the external backends, environments or generated output. We checked only source documentation, model metadata, and local device and dependency availability. No inference, installations, large downloads or paid requests were made. [Environment](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/Mira-Scene/blob/main/infer_scripts/docs/environment.md)

The published pipeline assembles object meshes using masks, depth and placement estimates. Its selected paths require NVIDIA-compatible drivers, CUDA extensions and separate dependencies such as SAM3, a depth backend and SAM3D or TRELLIS.2. Segmentation uses a service and a vision-language client in the documented default path. The optional environment-generation step needs service credentials. Single-image estimates cannot establish the hidden side of the crest or the position of the next bend. The paper discusses inaccurate masks, floaters and self-intersections. [Inference](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/Mira-Scene/blob/main/infer_scripts/README.md) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23796v1)

The viewer loads `scene.glb` or `scene_with_floor.glb`. Three.js can import that format. Individual rigid fittings could be evaluated on a suitable GPU host, with verified scale and alignment. This session produced no Mira geometry and integrated none. The delivered water, bank and casing correction was made on the existing Three.js scene and exported from its running renderer to editable Blender geometry.
