## What can be matched exactly

Each original fixes one camera view. At that view, align the existing camera and compare native frames with the same character pose, HUD and animation clock. ## How to judge a change

1. Start one experiment on its own branch from the newly checked specialist checkpoint. Capture Claudia’s baseline first. Change one identified cause so its effect can be seen in the comparison.
2. Use the exact approved opening, then replay the same camera, steering and shader clock from entrance to exit. Review both full turns, the major bend or drop, and the handoff. Check a short stretch before and after the correction for flicker, overlap or sudden changes.
3. Keep the full-size approved, before and candidate images separate. Measure the approved viewpoint on source pixels. For moving views, use the additional viewpoints you approve and the recorded footage. Reject a candidate that improves a single still while making the ride visibly worse.
4. Check all eight gameplay assertions, 91 coins, the 1,330–2,240 score progression, hearts and transitions. Measure sustained performance on a physical Android device before promoting a playable replacement. Each candidate stays isolated until you approve it.

The proposed causes above are hypotheses wherever a layer has not yet been isolated. This evidence-packaging session produced a repair plan and comparison media. It made no scenery correction or promotion.
