## What these tests establish

Bank height and clearance can be measured against Benny’s silhouette during steering. Ring centers, floor curvature and prop positions can be inspected in actual world coordinates. Blender meshes and materials can be exported through glTF and inspected in Three.js. At the final bend or plunge, turning off one rendering layer at a time distinguishes a physical obstruction from stretching in a painted texture. Cloud captures can detect those defects at the locked opening and through a continuous swim.

The original pictures supply one exact camera view per screen. The 20 depth and disagreement maps and ten saved camera or landmark files give constraints for fitting the scene. A monocular depth estimate leaves the shape behind objects and around bends ambiguous. The owner can approve particular full-size proposed viewpoints as additional design references.

The ten before-and-after movies cover each stage continuously on a fixed clock. Motion quicker than the five simulation steps between captured frames can escape the video. Sustained frame time, GPU load, touch behavior and heat through repeated swims require physical Android hardware. The native build used the Screen 06 source. Its role in this run was to verify the Android packaging path.

## Reusing the verified setup

The modeling tools run headlessly. The owner can request a run and inspect the results from Android. On a fresh cloud workspace, restore these exact pins in a disposable directory. Use the official Blender 5.3.0 archive and the single official Blender Lab MCP connection. The SDK archives, JDK verification, Gradle checksum, plugin pins and diagnostic results are in `checks.json`. The skill, camera fixtures and named capture poses provide the baseline for another run. [Saved motion skill and project instructions](https://github.com/josiahscalia5-dev/rainbow-/tree/3b57658f78fd5d7dce498d2743f1a09a173a570a)

For device verification, use a physical phone attached by authorized ADB to a trusted workstation or an available authorized physical-device service. Run the same branch and steering route through repeated complete swims. Record the device, Android and WebView versions, sustained frame times, temperature or thermal status, heart and coin totals, and touch response. The owner can review this evidence remotely. No device service was started for this run.

The native scene inventory and independent comparison results in this folder are small JSON files. The opening metrics here are the project comparator’s mean color error, hue correlation and edge correlation. The native whole-frame tolerance counts used in each experiment are under that screen’s `measurements/opening-metrics.json`.
