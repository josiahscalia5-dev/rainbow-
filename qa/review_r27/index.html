What gets every screen to 8.5 and beyond

  1. Keep the picture's own view while swimming. Every approved picture looks down the river from just above and behind Benny. When the view drops lower in motion, walls and rings take over. Holding that view keeps the river the centre of the screen.
  2. Carry each river's painted shape through the whole stage. The S-curves, width, crest and coin trail need to continue past the opening, not only appear in it.
  3. Paint what the picture never shows. For each screen, create a few extra views of the same world further along the route (like your three approved Screen 04 views), then paint them onto the 3D walls and water as Benny advances. This is the biggest single step toward 9/10.
  4. Give each screen its own foam. Soft, varied clusters shaped from that screen's picture, never rows of identical beads.
  5. One combined build. Each screen's best version currently sits in a separate work copy. Merge them, with the approved Benny on every screen, into one playable preview, then one Android build for your phone.
