## What produced the white side

The original water mesh runs from a raised rim down to an almost vertical skirt. A separate foam mesh rises above that rim and drops farther below it. There is also a white contact line drawn directly on the water at the old rim. Lowering only the foam exposed a bright ledge. An independent frozen render painting the water mesh green identified the second white line, and isolating its shader removed it.

The test gives both meshes one curve in the existing course’s coordinate system. It uses Claudia’s own rectified magenta water texture on the inclined strip, fits rounded foam along its outside edge and moves the white material contact there. The original cyan river surface between the banks carries its existing colors and swimming behavior. The changes occupy the first twelve units of the chamber visible through the outlet, with a gradual transition at both ends. They are enabled only for the isolated Screen 07 test. The upstream near-water work, coin path and camera use the retained specialist source.

The original Screen 07 and its artist-approved camera were checked against the repository reference digest. Claudia’s Account 17 recovery patch includes unfinished changes around the last five units before the chamber. This trial starts on the far side of that boundary at the chamber mouth. Her saved edits are preserved as a separate recovery patch.

In the real swim, compare 11.58 seconds near the plunge bottom and 12.08 seconds just inside Screen 08. More of the cyan and magenta bend comes into view on the left. The pale stripe and long water highlights farther up the drop are still visible. The outlet has an angular pink tip where the foam changes direction. This candidate addresses only the outlet.

## Remaining differences

Higher in the plunge, the streaks are still long and too vertical. The downstream wall also looks more uniformly pink and brighter than the deep purple metal in the original. Tight bends still show an abrupt angular change of direction. The next structural step is to smooth the course tangent used by the visible bank and fit its cross-sections through the bend. The reviewed geometry controls only the visible bank. Gameplay would continue to use the retained course and collision limits.

The real mesh check sampled 42 water and 52 foam edge vertices through the full-strength part of the outlet. Both met the designed curve within 0.00002 game units. At the plunge bottom, 34,995 pale pixels were attributable to visible foam in the retained diagnosis and 24,256 in the trial. A separate water-layer toggle counted 2,300 and 2,715. The earlier rejected foam-only experiment had exposed 9,558 such water pixels. These counts cover the same 432 × 768 viewpoint and lighting. They measure where the white material comes from.
